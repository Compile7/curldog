# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Moniwork to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Moniwork.

## What we don't accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through [a private security advisory](https://github.com/Compile7/moniwork/security/advisories/new) (it reaches maintainers only) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
